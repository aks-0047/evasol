## Rooftop solar with central financial assistance.

A Government of India (MNRE) scheme for residential rooftop solar. Eligibility, as published: an Indian household that owns a roof suitable for solar, has a valid electricity connection, and has not availed another solar subsidy.

**₹30,000/kW**\
up to 2 kW

**₹18,000**\
for the additional kW (up to 3 kW)

**Max ₹78,000**\
for systems of 3 kW and above

Journey: register on the national portal → feasibility approval → choose a registered vendor → installation → inspection and commissioning → subsidy claim. We can help you through the steps.

Per its company profile, Bhagavat Powertech has facilitated subsidy disbursement for 150+ customers across renewable energy projects.

Subsidy depends on system size, eligibility and approval; it is not guaranteed and does not make a project free. Commercial and industrial connections are not covered. States may add their own support. Confirm current terms at pmsuryaghar.gov.in.
